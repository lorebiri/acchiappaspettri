From the instrument file to a clean plot and analysis. Free, on your computer.

Online or desktop?

They are the same program: same file readers, same calculations, same numbers. Every version is tested by comparing the two on the same files (peaks, areas, suggestions, hypotheses and checks must match). What changes is the way you work.

Choose online if…

  • you want a quick look at a file, on any computer;
  • you cannot install programs (institute computer, classroom, library);
  • you want to try before deciding.

Choose desktop if…

  • you work with spectra every day or with series of many samples;
  • you have large files (maps, long chromatograms, millions of points);
  • you work offline, in the lab or on a field campaign;
  • you want to open instrument files with a double click and export straight into a folder.

How it works: load, review, download

Three steps, online and in the desktop program. Every choice can be undone and the original file is never modified.

  1. Load

    Drag single files, many files or a whole folder. The instrument is recognised and the plot is ready at once; a saved project brings back all the work.

  2. Review

    Peaks found, suggested names, data-quality check with one-click remedies, spectrum adjustments and analysis. Every option says whether it applies to the spectrum, the instrument or all plots.

  3. Download

    Tick what you need (PDF, images, peaks, processed data, analysis tables, traceability, project) and choose the file names: one button.

Peaks

Automatic peaks, by position and with a name

Peaks are found automatically, with a threshold from «many, even small ones» to «few, only the large ones». A click on an empty spot adds one, a click on a peak names it.

  • New By position: type one or more positions in the axis unit (1086; 712; 282), with optional width and shape (1500 w=10 lor) and snapping to the nearest maximum. A preview shows how the text was understood.
  • Names from libraries: suggestions from databases per sample type (cultural heritage, biological, chemistry, materials) and from your own libraries per instrument. They are always hypotheses to be confirmed.
  • Edit, delete, Ctrl+Z to undo; everything is saved in the project and goes into the peak CSV.

New · Adjust the spectrum

Adjustments and background removal, with a preview

Conversions (transmittance and absorbance, axis units), smoothing, cosmic rays, derivatives, normalisation and scaling. And now background removal with methods established in the literature.

  • Methods: arPLS, ALS, iterative polynomial (IModPoly, ModPoly), SNIP, rolling ball, convex hull, straight line, or anchor points picked on the plot or typed.
  • Live preview: while you move the sliders you see the spectrum before correction and the estimated background; recommended values are computed from the spectrum.
  • For one spectrum or all spectra of the technique; undo and «Restore the original». The original file never changes.
  • Method and parameters in the project, in the traceability file and in the methods draft, with references.

Data-quality check

Data quality, before the numbers

Before any analysis the program looks at the data and says what is wrong: noise, saturation, cosmic rays, missing points, irregular axis, drift, suspicious units, unread rows.

  • One-click remedies where they exist, with an explanation of what changes.
  • Technique-specific warnings (for example HPLC with a flat top, EPR with a curved baseline, XRF with few counts).
  • Warnings stay in the PDF and in the exported tables.

Analysis

Deconvolution of overlapping bands

Splits overlapping bands into Gaussian, Lorentzian or pseudo-Voigt components, on the region you choose or with a right click on the plot.

  • Uncertainty of every number: position, width, area and fraction with their error.
  • Number of components suggested and checked; the same deconvolution on several spectra desktop.
  • Validated on synthetic cases: right number of components in 197 cases out of 200, median area error +0.02 %.

Quantitation

Calibration and HPLC libraries

Ordinary or weighted calibration lines (1/x, 1/x²), with limits of detection and quantitation, Mandel test, confidence intervals and the status of every estimate (extrapolated, below LOD or LOQ, flat top, co-eluted…).

  • Standards, blank and control with recovery; PLS models for overlapping bands.
  • Multichannel HPLC: every channel of the instrument files, one chromatogram per channel.
  • Libraries per instrument and sample type that remember calibrations (slope, intercept, range, LOD, LOQ, date and batch): the concentration is computed without redoing the line, with the source next to every number.
  • Control chart, system suitability and maintenance in the library.

Comparison

Mixtures and reference spectra

Compare a spectrum with your references (similarity index) and decompose it into a mixture of references, with fractions and residual.

  • References loaded from files or taken from the open spectra; personal library to export and import.
  • Difference spectra, overlaid or offset comparisons with a legend.
  • Validated: fractions within 0.02 points (median), no false component in 1200 cases.

Several samples

Statistics: PCA, groups, MCR-ALS and kinetics

When there are many samples: PCA with score and loading plots, clustering with a dendrogram and a suggested number of groups, the trend of a band over time or with dose.

  • MCR-ALS on spectral series (over time, with dose, with temperature): SIMPLISMA, EFA, non-negativity, closure, unimodality, known spectra.
  • Global kinetic fit: order 0, 1 and 2, A → B → C, A ⇌ B, parallel reactions, with standard errors, residuals and AIC/BIC/F comparison.
  • Results in the analysis tables and in the PDF; settings in the project.

X-ray fluorescence

XRF: elements and composition

Energy calibration checked and corrected, elements recognised with coinciding lines declared (for example Pb Lα and As Kα), net areas with the SNIP background.

  • Standardless composition with fundamental parameters, always presented as an estimate with the assumptions used and a sensitivity range.
  • With your standards, concentrations and thicknesses.
  • For cultural heritage: probable elements and compatible pigments, as hypotheses.

Porous materials

BET and adsorption

Physisorption isotherms with both branches, BET surface area with the Rouquerol criteria (ISO 9277), Langmuir, pore volume, t-plot and αs, BJH and Dollimore–Heal, DR and DA, isosteric heat, DFT with your own kernel.

  • Chemisorption: TPR, TPO and TPD with Kissinger and Redhead, pulses, static chemisorption, dispersion and metal area.
  • Guided import when the file's columns are uncertain.
  • Results in the CSV and the PDF, settings in the project.

Results

Exports: PDF, images, tables, methods

One window: choose the items, the name of each file and the spectra included. Presets remember your choices.

  • PDF ready to read, with several plots per page, comparison pages and analysis pages.
  • Images: PNG at 600 dpi and vector SVG, with journal sizes.
  • CSV for Excel: peaks, processed data, original data, analysis tables; workbook for Origin and .opj/.opju projects.
  • Draft «Methods» paragraph, in English or Italian, with the processing actually used and references.
  • Traceability: the fingerprint of every file and every processing step, to reproduce every number.

Projects

One file keeps data and work

The .acchspettri project contains the original data of every spectrum (binary formats included) and all the work: names, peaks, processing, deconvolutions, calibrations, references, libraries, comparisons, settings.

  • Reopen it on another computer, online or on the desktop, or pass it to a colleague.
  • Checked at every opening; documented, open format.
  • On the desktop: saved to a file, recent projects, double click to open.

Desktop program

Comparisons, detachable windows, dark theme

On the desktop, plots are compared overlaid or offset and can be detached into separate windows («Detach», «Comparison»), also on a second screen. The fast WebGL plot stays smooth with millions of points.

  • Menus with every shortcut, command bar (Ctrl+K), multi-selection.
  • Status bar with the processing chain of the active spectrum.
  • Light or dark theme; exported plots stay white.

Reliability: numbers you can check

Scientific software must say how far its numbers can be trusted. This is how Acchiappaspettri does it.

Validated against public references

Every calculation is compared with a known truth: the NIST reference problems for regression, synthetic spectra generated with a known truth, public data. The tests run automatically at every change, hundreds per version.

Open documents

Validation results, scientific choices, known limitations and the project format are public, together with the code: validation, science, limitations, project format (in Italian).

«Hypotheses» and «estimates» declared

Automatic identifications are presented as hypotheses to be confirmed; concentrations without standards as estimates, with the assumptions used. A single band is inconclusive, and the program says so.

  • −0.3 %median area error of Lorentzian peaks with the fit (against −17.4 % valley to valley)
  • 197 / 200deconvolutions with the right number of components, on bands 0.7–1.5 widths apart
  • 0.56 %median background error with IModPoly on 100 synthetic Raman spectra with fluorescence
  • 94.9 %of the 95 % confidence intervals of the slope contain the true value

Tests on certified materials have not been done yet: calibration remains the responsibility of whoever measures, as with any software.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost? Is there a paid version?

Nothing, for everyone: universities, research institutes, companies, schools. There is no paid version and no subscription. It is free software under the GNU GPL version 3 or later: you can use, redistribute and modify it.

Are my data uploaded anywhere?

No. Even online, files are read and processed in your browser, on your computer: they are not sent to any server. The desktop program connects to the internet only to check for updates.

Do online and desktop give the same results?

Yes: they use the same readers and the same calculations. Where the desktop uses the native Rust engine, the numbers are identical to the reference ones (bit for bit or within 1·10⁻¹² of the scale), and every version checks this with automatic tests.

How do I check the SHA-256 fingerprint of the downloaded file?
  • Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash .\file-name.exe
  • macOS (Terminal): shasum -a 256 file-name.dmg
  • Linux: sha256sum file-name

The result must match the fingerprint shown next to the file in the Download section.

Why is the program not signed?

A code-signing certificate costs money every year and the project has no budget: it prefers to stay free for everyone. The code is public, the packages are built automatically from the code on GitHub, the fingerprints are published and updates are signed and verified by the program.

How does it update?

The desktop program checks whether a new version is out and offers it: it installs with one click, after verifying its signature. The online version is always the latest.

My instrument is not in the list: can I still use it?

Almost always: nearly every instrument exports column text (CSV, TXT) or JCAMP-DX, which the program reads. If the proprietary format is not read, send an example file; if you can program, you can also write an .acchplugin extension without code.

Can I trust the suggested peak names?

They are hypotheses based on band positions, useful to get oriented: they must be confirmed with other bands, other techniques or reference samples. That is why the program calls them hypotheses and keeps them apart from the names you confirm.

How do I cite it in a paper or thesis?

The repository has a CITATION.cff file with the citation. The draft «Methods» paragraph exported by the program describes the processing used, with the references for the methods.

What does my computer need?

Online: an up-to-date browser. Desktop: Windows 10 or 11 64-bit, macOS 11 or later on a Mac with Apple chip or Intel, 64-bit Linux (x86_64). No terminal or configuration needed. On Windows the program uses the WebView2 system component, already present on Windows 11 and almost always on Windows 10: if it is missing, the installer downloads it (an internet connection is needed in that case).

I bookmarked the online version: does anything change?

No: the online version stays at the same address, lorebiri.github.io/acchiappaspettri. This presentation page lives in a separate section.

Privacy, contact and reports

Privacy

No account, no cookies, no analytics. Spectral data never leave the computer. This page only asks GitHub for the list of files of the latest version. GitHub, which hosts the pages, keeps the usual technical access logs, like any website.

Report a problem

In the program: Help → Report a problem… prepares a message in your email program with technical data and a screenshot you can black out. It sends nothing by itself and includes no spectral data without your consent.
